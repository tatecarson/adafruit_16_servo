S0–S2 are the three wand throws and the motor turns the ring. Spread fans the wands apart; at 0 all three sit on one bearing and the tubes get struck in a flam. Wands rest clear reads 100 %down (the unpowered rest) as fully raised — without it the machine idles with every wand buried in the orbit, hammering.

Dashboard link

waiting for servo_controller.html…
Mirrors every command the dashboard sends, over a same-origin BroadcastChannel. Open both pages from the same origin — 127.0.0.1 and localhost are different origins and will not talk. RUN/ROTATE/STOP/MOTION are cluster-wide and reach all three machines; UP/DOWN/DMOVE are board-local and reach the board they name. A command the dashboard could not map to a board goes to the whole room.
